Easy recipe for American pancakes

Make traditional American pancakes with this easy and delicious pancake recipe.

Ingredients
Portions: 4

Instructions

  1. Mix together flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Slowly pour in half of the milk while whisking until it's a smooth batter.
  3. Whisk in the rest of the milk and the eggs.
  4. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes.
  5. Fry the pancakes in a little bit of butter in a pan.
  6. Serve pancakes with maple syrup, berries or fruit.
